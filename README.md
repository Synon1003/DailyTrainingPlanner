## Daily Training Planner
A Blazor WASM PWA written in .Net 10.  
A lightweight client application, can be installed on your pc/mobile device via browser from [Azure](https://ambitious-island-023363703.3.azurestaticapps.net/).

#### Functions
- You can pick up daily training checklists for the next 2 weeks
- Your checklist will be stored in your browser's local storage
